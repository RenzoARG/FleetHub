Fleet codes are self-contained strings that encode your entire fleet — ships, quantities, and metadata.

No server, no database. The code is the data. Share it anywhere: Discord, Reddit, forums, text messages.

The recipient pastes it in the Import tab and instantly sees your fleet reconstructed.

FORMAT: BASE64(version + ships + metadata)
CHECKSUM: CRC-style 4-char suffix
MAX SHIPS: 60+ unique hulls
